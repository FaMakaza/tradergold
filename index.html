BIADRE in gold sessions
Balance — Asian Session
22:00-08:00
Price consolidates in a defined range. Both buyers and sellers are in equilibrium. This is the accumulation phase. Mark the Asian High and Low. Institutions are positioning quietly. Do not trade during balance.
Tight balance range (under 80 pips) = aggressive imbalance incoming at London open.

Imbalance + Displacement — London
07:00-13:00
London breaks the balance. A strong directional candle (displacement) shows institutional commitment. Price moves aggressively away from balance, leaving an imbalance gap. This is the institutional direction signal.
The displacement candle must be strong: large body, minimal wick, closes near extreme. Weak candles = no institutional commitment.

Acceptance + Execution — Overlap
13:00-17:00
Price accepts the displaced level then retraces into the imbalance zone. This retracement is your execution point. Enter when price returns to the imbalance zone with a confirming candle. 60-70% of daily range forms here.
If price accepts for 2+ H1 candles before retracing, that is high-confidence institutional acceptance.
